Your prepared game files

Horizon doesn't ship Minecraft's files. Prepare them from your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition with horizon-prepare --prepare (it needs Java), then pick the folder it wrote, .local/horizon-client/26.3, or a zip of it (one Horizon exported on another device works too). They stay in this browser's storage for next time.

Add sound

Horizon takes the game's sounds from your own Minecraft. Pick the folder your launcher plays Minecraft 26.3 from (the official launcher's minecraft, or SKLauncher's or Prism's folder). Only the sounds are read; they stay in this browser's storage.

Your game files

This browser keeps the game files you imported. Export them as a zip to play on another device: pick the zip there instead of preparing again. The zip holds Minecraft's files from your own copy, so keep it to yourself.
